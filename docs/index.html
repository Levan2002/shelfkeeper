Help & FAQ

How do I get the best shelf scan?

Stand straight in front of the shelf, fill the frame with spines and use good, even light. Five to fifteen spines per photo works best. Books whose spines are hard to read (tiny type, foil on dark cloth) may appear under “Couldn't Identify”; tap Search to pick the right book, or scan its barcode.

A match is wrong. How do I fix it?

Matches marked “Check” are not selected by default. Touch and hold any result and choose “Not This Book…” to search for the right edition, or untick it before adding. After adding, open the book and use Edit Details.

How do I import from Goodreads?

On the Goodreads website open My Books › Import and export › Export Library and download the CSV. In Shelfkeeper open Settings › Import from Goodreads and choose the file. Shelves, ratings, read dates and reviews come across; covers download in the background. StoryGraph and LibraryThing exports in Goodreads format work too.

Can I export my library?

Yes. Settings › Export Library (CSV) creates a Goodreads-compatible file you can open in Numbers or Excel, or import elsewhere.

How does lending work?

Open a book and tap Lend This Book. Enter who is borrowing it and, optionally, a due date. The Lent Out tab lists every loan, with overdue ones first; swipe left when a book comes back. With Pro you can get a reminder at 10 AM on the due date.

How do Siri and Visual Intelligence work with Shelfkeeper?

Say “Do I own a book in Shelfkeeper?” or “Who has my book in Shelfkeeper?”. With Visual Intelligence, point your iPhone at a book cover (or take a screenshot of one) and Shelfkeeper's results show whether it's already in your library.

What does Pro include?

The free version includes up to 50 books and 3 shelf scans. Shelfkeeper Pro unlocks unlimited books, unlimited shelf scans and loan reminders. It's available as a yearly subscription with a 7-day free trial, a monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Restore purchases from Settings on a new iPhone.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open the Settings app › your name › Subscriptions › Shelfkeeper Pro. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, and in your iCloud backup if you use iCloud Backup. There is no Shelfkeeper account or server. See the privacy policy.
