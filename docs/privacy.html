Privacy Policy

Shelfkeeper: Home Library · Updated 30 September 2026

In short: Shelfkeeper has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your library stays on your iPhone. Photos are read on your iPhone and never uploaded.

Your library

Books, shelves, notes, ratings and loans (including the names of people you lend books to) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's private storage. If you use iCloud Backup, they are included in your own encrypted device backup. The developer has no server and cannot see this data.

Photos and camera

Photos of bookshelves and barcodes are processed on your iPhone with Apple's text recognition and, where available, Apple Intelligence. Photos are never uploaded or stored by the app beyond the scan (a cover photo you choose yourself is kept with that book on your iPhone).

Book lookups

To find book details and covers, Shelfkeeper sends the text of a title/author or an ISBN to Open Library (Internet Archive) and to Apple's public Apple Books search. No identifier, account or personal data is sent with these requests. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests: Internet Archive, Apple.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to confirm your Pro status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (which product, when, and its status) and a random anonymous app user ID generated on your device. This data is used only to provide the purchase and restore features. It is not linked to your identity, not used for tracking, and not sold.

Siri, Spotlight and Visual Intelligence

If you use them, iOS shows your book titles in Spotlight, Siri and Visual Intelligence results on your device. This happens within iOS; nothing is sent to the developer.

Notifications

Loan reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete books in the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.
